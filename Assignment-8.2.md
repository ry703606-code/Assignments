Assignment 8.2 — Auto-Responsive Image Gallery[cite: 37]
What to build: An auto-responsive image gallery[cite: 37].
Steps:

1. Collect at least 8 images[cite: 37].
2. Place each image inside a gallery item[cite: 37].
3. Use `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...))` on the grid[cite: 37].
4. Apply `width: 100%` and suitable `object-fit` for the images[cite: 37].
5. Keep consistent spacing using `gap`[cite: 37].
6. Verify that columns adjust automatically by changing the browser width[cite: 37].
Submission: Responsive photo gallery and desktop/mobile screenshots[cite: 37].
