Assignment 4.3 — Student Marks Table[cite: 18]
What to build: A student marksheet table.[cite: 18]
Steps:

1. Add data for at least 5 students and 3 subjects.[cite: 18]
2. Use `<table>`, `<tr>`, `<th>`, and `<td>` for the table.[cite: 18]
3. Organize the table into `<thead>`, `<tbody>`, and `<tfoot>`.[cite: 18]
4. Give a title to the table using `<caption>`.[cite: 18]
5. Show total or average marks in the footer.[cite: 18]
6. Use `colspan` or `rowspan` where appropriate.[cite: 18]
7. Keep table headers meaningful.[cite: 18]
Submission: `marksheet.html` and a screenshot of the complete table.[cite: 18]
