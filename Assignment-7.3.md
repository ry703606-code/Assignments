Assignment 7.3 — Responsive Product Listing
Kya banana hai: Product cards ki responsive listing.
Steps:

1. Kam se kam 6 product cards banao.
2. Har card mein image, name, price aur button add karo.
3. Container par display: flex aur flex-wrap: wrap use karo.
4. gap se cards ke beech spacing do.
5. flex-grow, flex-shrink aur flex-basis experiment karo.
6. Screen chhoti hone par cards ko next line mein wrap hona chahiye.
   Submission: Responsive product listing aur screenshot at desktop and mobile widths.
