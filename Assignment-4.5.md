Assignment 4.5 — Multi-Page College Club Website
Kya banana hai: Ek complete college club website with four pages.
Required pages:

- index.html — Home
- about.html — Club introduction
- registration.html — Club registration form
- contact.html — Contact details
  Steps:

1. Sabhi pages par consistent navigation banao.
2. Home page par club ka introduction aur activities show karo.
3. About page par club ki details aur members ki information do.
4. Registration page par student registration form banao.
5. Contact page par email, location aur contact information show karo.
6. Ek information table add karo, jaise events schedule.
7. Metadata aur favicon add karo.
8. Saare internal links test karo.
