Assignment 4.5 — Multi-Page College Club Website[cite: 20]
What to build: A complete college club website with four pages.[cite: 20]
Required pages:
- `index.html` — Home[cite: 20]
- `about.html` — Club introduction[cite: 20]
- `registration.html` — Club registration form[cite: 20]
- `contact.html` — Contact details[cite: 20]
Steps:

1. Build consistent navigation across all pages.[cite: 20]
2. Show club introduction and activities on the home page.[cite: 20]
3. Provide club details and member information on the about page.[cite: 20]
4. Build a student registration form on the registration page.[cite: 20]
5. Show email, location, and contact information on the contact page.[cite: 20]
6. Add an information table, such as an events schedule.[cite: 20]
7. Add metadata and a favicon.[cite: 20]
8. Test all internal links.[cite: 20]
